## Permit Silence
- **ID:** `2f3a3175-c27b-8115-99e3-c6041921eeef`
- **URL:** https://www.notion.so/Permit-Silence-2f3a3175c27b811599e3c6041921eeef
- **Created:** 2026-01-25T20:17:00.000Z
- **Edited:** 2026-01-25T20:39:00.000Z
- **Unique insight:** 
• The Signal: If a home was built in 2005 and has no permit for a roof replacement in the last 20 years, the probability of a roof failure is near 100%. Similarly, an HVAC system in the South (Greensboro) typically lasts 12-15 years. "Silence" in the mechanical permit data for a 16-year-old home is a strong "buy" signal for a contractor.
• The Moat: As correctly identified, the moat is the difficulty of aggregating this data. While national aggregators like BuildFax exist, their data is often lagged or lacks the granularity of specific trade permits (mechanical vs. structural) in smaller municipalities. In Greensboro, permit data is fragmented across city and county portals (Open Gate City, Guilford County Civic Access), often requiring specific scraping and standardization that national players overlook.
• Differentiation: Unlike "Ambulance Chasing" leads (storm damage), these are "Silent" leads. The homeowner may not even know they need a replacement yet. This allows a contractor to market proactively ("Your roof is statistically likely to fail this year") rather than reactively.
- **Customer:** All trades, vendors of home equipment, investors, insurance co, lenders, wholesalers, marketing firms.
- **Industry:** PM Software
- **Status:** Ideation
- **Solution:** Building a proprietary dataset that utilizes the "silence" (absence) of permits combined with the "Year Built" of a property to predict the imminent failure of major systems (HVAC, Roof, Water Heater). Value Proposition: Creating high-intent, exclusive leads for contractors and "CapEx Risk" intelligence for real estate investors.
- **Market size:** $6B
- **Problem:** Lack of data about the age and conditional of homes systems. HVAC, roof, electrical, plumbing, water heater, remodeled status
- **Business type:** B2B
